# Support

**Email [support@subti.app](mailto:support@subti.app).** One person reads it, and answers.

## Try this first

**[subti.app/support](https://subti.app/support)** covers what almost every support email turns out
to be, and most of it takes under a minute: VLC not being detected, subtitles not appearing, the
Accessibility permission, model downloads, Ollama connection, silent audio clips, licence recovery
and updates.

## Writing in

Include:

- Your **Subti version and build** — both are in the menu bar.
- Your **macOS version** and Mac model.
- Your **VLC version**, if the problem involves playback, subtitles or the overlay.
- What you did, what happened, and what you expected instead.

That is usually enough to spot the problem on the first reply.

Please do not send films, subtitle files, dictionary data or anything else private. If a particular
file is the only way to reproduce something, I will ask, and say exactly what I need.

## Licence and purchase

- **Lost your key.** Email from the address you bought with and I will send it again.
- **A key that will not activate** is nearly always a stray space from copying. Paste it, then delete
  anything before or after it.
- **A new Mac.** One licence is for one person, active on up to 2 Macs at a time. Choose *Deactivate
  this Mac* in Settings on the one you are replacing, then paste the same key on the new one.
  Reinstalling on a Mac that is already activated needs nothing.

## Bugs and requests

Open an [issue](https://github.com/troshkinpavel/subti-releases/issues) here, or email — whichever
suits you. The issue tracker on this repository is for the app and its releases; the source code is
not public, so there is nothing to send a pull request against.

**Security problems are different.** Please report those privately instead: [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
